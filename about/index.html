About Maser Construction

Maser Construction is a Calgary-based project management and design firm. Our team combines architectural drafting, MEP coordination and construction project management, with more than 15 years of combined experience.

How we work

  • We confirm what your permit requires before we quote
  • We prepare architectural and MEP drawings together so they agree
  • We set out scope, schedule and fees clearly
  • We stay involved through review comments, inspections and handover

Who we work with

Business owners and tenants opening or updating a space, landlords and property managers, homeowners planning permitted renovations, and contractors who need drawings and permits handled.

Stamping and credentials

Stamping is carried out by professionals registered in the province or territory of your project. We confirm who that is, and what is required, before we quote.

Where we work

Our office is in Calgary, Alberta, and we serve projects across Canada. See our Calgary page and the areas we serve, or browse all services.

Let's talk about your project

Tell us what you are building. We will confirm what your permit needs and give you a clear plan and quote.
