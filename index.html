Frequently asked questions

What does Maser Construction do?

We prepare permit-ready drawings, arrange architectural and MEP stamping through registered professionals, support permit applications and provide project management. We are based in Calgary, Alberta, and serve projects across Canada.

Do you work outside Calgary?

Yes. We serve projects across Canada. Building codes and stamping rules differ by province, so we confirm the requirements for your location before we quote.

Do I need stamped drawings for a permit?

It depends on your project type, size, province and municipality. Commercial and larger projects often require them. Our guide explains the factors, and we can confirm the requirement for your project.

How do I get started?

Send us a short description of your project or call 778-803-5408. We review the scope, confirm what your permit needs and give you a clear plan and quote.

Ready to start your project?

Tell us what you are building. We will confirm what your permit needs and give you a clear plan and quote.
