Calgary permit questions

Do I need a permit for a tenant improvement in Calgary?

Often yes, especially when the work changes layout, building systems, or the use of the space. Requirements depend on the scope, so we confirm them with the City of Calgary's current guidance before we start.

Where do I apply for a building permit in Calgary?

Building permit applications in Calgary go through the City of Calgary. With the owner's authorization, we prepare the application package and can handle submission and follow-up.

Do you serve areas around Calgary?

Yes. We work on projects in Calgary, the surrounding municipalities and across Alberta, as well as in other provinces.
