Interior Design: common questions

Can I hire you for interior design only, without a permit project?

Yes. Interior design is available on its own — for a refresh, a fit-out or a renovation that does not need a permit. If your project does need a permit, we coordinate the design with the permit drawing set so nothing has to be redrawn.

What do you need from me to start a design?

The address, measurements or existing drawings if you have them, photos of the space, and a short description of the style and function you want. From there we confirm the scope and give you a clear fee before design work begins.

Do you design interiors outside my city?

Yes. We work remotely across Canada — consultations, concepts and design packages are handled by phone, email and video call, and the finished design is delivered as drawings and selections your local contractor can build from.
