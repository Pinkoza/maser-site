Project Management & Coordination: common questions

Who does the construction work?

Licensed contractors carry out the construction, whether you already have one or we help you tender the work. Our role is to plan, coordinate and keep the project on the approved design, schedule and budget.

Can you manage projects outside Calgary?

Yes, we can coordinate remotely and through local contractors and consultants. We confirm how site oversight will work for your location before you commit.

When should I involve a project manager?

Early. Planning the budget, schedule and permit steps together avoids surprises later.
