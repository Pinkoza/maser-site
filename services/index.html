Drafting, design & stamping — the whole permit package

Maser Construction is a drafting company with stamping services. Hire us for the complete package — drafting, MEP coordination, code and zoning checks, interior design, stamping and the assembled permit submission — or for any single service on its own.

The whole package, in seven steps

One contract, one team, from first drawing to permit-ready submission. You do not need a separate drafting firm, a separate code reviewer and a separate stamping provider — and you do not have to coordinate between them.

  1. Drafting / design drawingsArchitectural set: site plan, floor plans, elevations, sections, details and schedules.
  2. MEP drafting & coordinationMechanical / HVAC, electrical and plumbing drawings coordinated so disciplines do not clash at review.
  3. Code & zoning checkNational and provincial code review, zoning, occupancy, fire separations and exits — checked before submission, not after a deficiency letter.
  4. Interior designSpace planning, layouts, finishes, lighting and millwork for tenant improvements and fit-outs.
  5. Review, coordination & stampingArchitectural and MEP stamping through registered professionals; province rules confirmed before we quote.
  6. Permit package & supportThe assembled submission set with schedules and forms, plus one round of permit deficiency responses included on stamped sets.
  7. Project managementOne point of contact through revisions to permit.

Every service below is part of the package — or available on its own. Tell us about your project and we will confirm what it needs, with a clear scope, schedule and fee before work starts.
