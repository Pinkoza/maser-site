Architectural and MEP stamping for permit applications

Many permit applications require drawings that are stamped, or sealed, by a registered professional. The stamp means a licensed architect or engineer has reviewed the work and takes professional responsibility for it.

We coordinate the review and stamping of architectural and MEP drawings so your submission meets the requirement the first time. Stamping rules differ by province and by project type, so we confirm what applies to your project before we quote.

Architectural & MEP Stamping: common questions

What does it mean to stamp drawings?

A stamp, or seal, shows that a registered architect or engineer has reviewed the drawings and accepts professional responsibility for them. Authorities use it to confirm that qualified professionals have signed off on the design.

Who is allowed to stamp drawings?

Only professionals registered with the regulator in the province or territory where the project is located. Requirements vary by province, project type and building size, which is why we confirm them at the start.

Do you stamp projects outside Alberta?

We confirm the stamping requirements and the registered professional needed for your province before we quote, so you know what is possible and what it costs before you commit.
