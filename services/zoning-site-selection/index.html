Zoning checks and site selection before you lease or buy

The most expensive permit problems are the ones found after the lease is signed. A zoning check tells you early whether a property allows your intended use and what it will take to open.

We review the property and the applicable rules, and give you a clear summary you can use in negotiations with the landlord or seller.

Zoning Checks & Site Selection: common questions

Why check zoning before signing a lease?

A property may not allow your use, or may require extra approvals or upgrades. Checking first lets you negotiate, or walk away, before you are committed.

Does a zoning check guarantee a permit?

No. It identifies the rules and the main risks. The permit authority makes the final decision, so we describe what we find and what remains uncertain.

Can you compare several sites?

Yes. We can review a shortlist and summarize how each one fits your use and timeline.
