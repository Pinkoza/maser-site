Permit Support & Applications: common questions

Can you apply for the permit on my behalf?

In most cases yes, with the owner's authorization. Some authorities require the owner or contractor to apply directly, and we guide you through those steps.

What happens if the authority sends review comments?

We review each comment, revise the drawings where needed and resubmit, so the response is complete and clear.

Can you guarantee approval or a timeline?

No. Timelines and decisions belong to the authority. We prepare a complete, well-organized submission and follow up, which gives your application its best chance.
