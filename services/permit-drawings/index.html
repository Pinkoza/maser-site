Permit Drawings: common questions

What is included in a permit drawing set?

A typical set includes a site plan, floor plans, elevations, sections, details and schedules, with code and zoning information noted. Larger or more complex projects also include structural and MEP drawings. We confirm the exact list after reviewing your scope and your municipality's requirements.

Do I need drawings for a small renovation?

It depends on the scope and your local rules. Work that changes layout, structure or building systems often needs a permit and drawings, while cosmetic work often does not. Your municipality can confirm, and we can help you find out before you spend money.

How long do permit drawings take?

Timing depends on project size, how much existing information is available and how quickly decisions are made. We give you an estimated schedule after we review your scope, and we will say plainly if a deadline is unrealistic.
