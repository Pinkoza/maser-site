Ventilation design & HVAC drawings for a Calgary care-home conversion

An existing two-storey house in Calgary was being converted into a home-type care residence — and its building permit stalled when the City's mechanical reviewer issued a deficiency. We were engaged to produce the ventilation design and HVAC drawings needed to answer it.

Get a free quoteCall 778-803-5408

The situation

  • Existing 2,306 sq ft two-storey house, converted to a Group B, Division 4 home-type care occupancy — four residents and a live-in caregiver
  • City of Calgary mechanical review issued a deficiency on the building permit in November 2026
  • The examiner required HVAC drawings showing all ventilation — ducts, duct sizing, air volumes and outdoor air rates — meeting ASHRAE 62-2016, plus a point-by-point response with the resubmission
  • The original HVAC contractor was out of business and no mechanical drawings existed — the design had to be built from the architectural set and the equipment actually installed

What we delivered

  • Ventilation design and calculations: room-by-room supply, return and exhaust schedule, with outdoor air rates calculated to ASHRAE 62-2016
  • HRV sizing check against the unit actually installed — site photos showed it differed from the model named on the plans, and the drawings were corrected to match
  • Full HVAC drawing set: duct layouts for the main and upper floors and the basement mechanical room, every run sized and labelled, register and grille locations and sizes, and air volumes shown on the plans
  • Bathroom, kitchen and dryer exhaust rates, and the point-by-point response letter the City requires with the resubmission

How it ran

  1. ReviewWe read the deficiency letter against the submitted plans and confirmed exactly what the examiner needed.
  2. QuoteFixed fee confirmed before work started — no hourly billing, one round of examiner revisions included.
  3. DeliverDesign, calculations and drawings completed on a 3–5 business day schedule for the resubmission.
