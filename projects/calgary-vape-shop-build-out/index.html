Retail build-out drawings for a Calgary vape shop

A small retail unit in Calgary was being fitted out as a vape shop — a classic leasehold improvement: a tight floor plan, a landlord's base building, and a City permit clock running. We were engaged for the architectural and engineering drawing package behind the build-out.

Get a free quoteCall 778-803-5408

The project

  • Leasehold improvement of an existing retail unit in Calgary — new retail use fitted into a landlord's shell space
  • Scope: architectural and engineering services for the build-out — floor plan, layouts and the permit drawing package
  • Small footprint, but the same permit path as a large project: drawings, code check, submission, and responses while the City processed the application

How we worked

  • Drafting worked directly with the owner on the floor plan — small retail layouts live or die on counter runs, storage and code clearances fitting at once
  • Revisions turned around quickly as the plan was refined, so City processing time was not lost to communication delays
  • One fixed engagement covering the drawing package — no hourly surprises on a small job

Small job, same discipline

Leasehold improvements are the bread and butter of small retail — and the projects most often hurt by treating the permit set as an afterthought. A build-out drawn properly the first time opens on schedule; one that bounces at the City costs rent on a space that cannot open. That is the whole case for our permit drawings service at this size.
