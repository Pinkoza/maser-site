We serve projects in every province and territory. Much of our work, from drawings to permit preparation, can be done remotely, with local coordination where a project needs it.

Tell us about your project

Every province has its own rules

Each province and territory adopts or adapts the National Building Code through its own building code, such as the Alberta Building Code, the British Columbia Building Code, the Ontario Building Code and the Quebec Construction Code. Municipalities then add their own permit processes and forms.

Stamping must be done by professionals registered in the province or territory where the project is located. That is why we confirm the requirements for your location, and the registered professional you need, before we quote.

Working across provinces: questions

Can you handle a project in another province?

Yes. We work remotely with local contractors and consultants, and we confirm the building code and stamping requirements for your province before we quote.

Why do requirements differ between provinces?

Each province and territory adopts or adapts the National Building Code through its own building code, and professional regulators set their own rules for who may stamp drawings.

How does site coordination work for my location?

We confirm at the start how oversight will work for your location, usually through your contractor and local consultants, supported by regular reviews of progress and drawings.

Have a project anywhere in Canada?

Tell us what you are building. We will confirm what your permit needs and give you a clear plan and quote.
