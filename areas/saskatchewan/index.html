What we do in Saskatchewan

  • Permit Drawings: Complete architectural drawing sets prepared for your permit application.
  • Architectural & MEP Stamping: Review and stamping by licensed professionals where your permit requires it.
  • MEP Drafting & Coordination: Mechanical, electrical and plumbing drawings coordinated with the architecture.
  • Zoning Checks & Site Selection: Know what a property allows before you lease or buy.
  • Permit Support & Applications: We prepare, submit and follow up on your permit application.
  • Project Management & Coordination: Planning, budgets, schedules and contractor coordination from permit to handover.

Stamping must be done by professionals registered in the province or territory of the project, so we confirm who that is, and what your project requires, at the start. See how we work across Canada.

Saskatchewan permit questions

Do I need stamped drawings in Saskatchewan?

Often for commercial and larger projects, but it depends on the building type, size and scope of work. We confirm the requirement for your project in Saskatchewan before we quote.

Which building code applies in Saskatchewan?

Projects in Saskatchewan follow the National Building Code of Canada as adopted in Saskatchewan, along with municipal bylaws and zoning. We check what applies to your project and location.

Can you work on projects anywhere in Saskatchewan?

Yes. We prepare drawings and permit documents remotely and coordinate with your local contractor and consultants. See the cities listed on this page, or contact us about your location.
