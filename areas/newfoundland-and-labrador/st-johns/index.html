How permits work in St. John's

Building permits in St. John's are handled by the City of St. John's, and projects follow the National Building Code of Canada as adopted in Newfoundland and Labrador together with local zoning. Requirements differ by building type, use and scope of work, and they change over time, so we check the current requirements for every project instead of relying on a template.

That is why we start each St. John's project by confirming what your permit needs: which drawings, whether stamping is required, and how long to plan for.

St. John's permit questions

Do I need a permit for a renovation in St. John's?

Often yes when the work changes layout, structure or building systems, or changes the use of a space. Requirements depend on your scope, so we confirm them with the City of St. John's before drawings begin.

Do I need stamped drawings in St. John's?

It depends on the building type, size and scope of work, and on the rules in Newfoundland & Labrador. We confirm the requirement for your project before we quote.

Can you work with contractors in St. John's?

Yes. We work remotely and coordinate with your St. John's contractor and local consultants, and we confirm at the start how site coordination will work.
