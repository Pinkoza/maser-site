How permits work in Montréal

Building permits in Montréal are handled by the Ville de Montréal, and projects follow the Quebec Construction Code together with local zoning. Requirements differ by building type, use and scope of work, and they change over time, so we check the current requirements for every project instead of relying on a template.

That is why we start each Montréal project by confirming what your permit needs: which drawings, whether stamping is required, and how long to plan for.

Montréal permit questions

Do I need a permit for a renovation in Montréal?

Often yes when the work changes layout, structure or building systems, or changes the use of a space. Requirements depend on your scope, so we confirm them with the Ville de Montréal before drawings begin.

Do I need stamped drawings in Montréal?

It depends on the building type, size and scope of work, and on the rules in Quebec. We confirm the requirement for your project before we quote.

Can you work with contractors in Montréal?

Yes. We work remotely and coordinate with your Montréal contractor and local consultants, and we confirm at the start how site coordination will work.
